## 2. Your Lead Pipeline
- Navigate to **Leads (CRM)** to view prospective buyers and tenants assigned to you.
- **Stage Progression:**
  - `New`: Uncontacted inquiries.
  - `Contacted`: Initial phone call or WhatsApp message sent.
  - `Qualified`: Verified budget, time horizon, and purchasing intent.
  - `Visit Scheduled`: On-site viewing arranged.
  - `Negotiation`: Token or offer submitted.
  - `Converted`: Deal signed.
- Click any lead to append follow-up consultation notes.

---

## 3. Scheduled Viewings & On-Site Visits
- View all your confirmed property tours under **Visits**.
- Review client contact details, meeting location, and visitor headcounts.
- When a visit concludes, click **Complete** and enter the client's impressions and feedback to keep your deal pipeline up to date.

---

## 4. Closing Deals
- Track offer amounts and agreed purchase prices in **Deals**.
- Update closing dates and note token payment details.
