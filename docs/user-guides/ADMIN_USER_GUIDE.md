# AQARFLOW — AGENCY ADMINISTRATOR USER GUIDE
### Master Operations Manual for Agency Principals and Office Managers

Welcome to **AqarFlow** by **VIRELEO**. As an Agency Administrator, you have complete authority over your organization's property inventory, customer pipeline, agent scheduling, deals, and agency configuration.

---

## 2. Managing Properties & Inventory
1. Navigate to **Properties** in the left sidebar.
2. Click **Add Property** to register a new listing.
3. Enter key details:
   - **Reference Code:** Use standard agency formatting (e.g. `AF-007`). Duplicate reference codes are strictly prevented.
   - **Purpose:** For Sale / For Rent / Commercial Lease.
   - **Pricing:** Enter price in PKR (Pakistani Rupees).
   - **Specs:** Bedrooms, bathrooms, area value, and area measurement unit (Marla, Kanal, Sqft, Sqyd).
   - **City & Locality:** Islamabad (F-7, F-8, DHA), Lahore (DHA, Gulberg, Bahria), Karachi (Clifton, DHA), Rawalpindi.
4. **Marketplace Publication:** Toggle the **Published** switch to make the listing instantly discoverable on the public portal.

---

## 3. Lead Management & Agent Assignment
- Every inquiry submitted by public visitors on the website immediately appears in your CRM with the status `New`.
- Switch between **Pipeline (Kanban)** and **Table** view.
- Click on any lead to open their profile drawer:
  - **Assign Agent:** Select any registered agent to route the client to them.
  - **Stage Update:** Move leads through `New` &rarr; `Contacted` &rarr; `Qualified` &rarr; `Visit Scheduled` &rarr; `Negotiation` &rarr; `Converted` &rarr; `Lost`.
  - **Activity & Notes:** Record consultation notes, financing details, or buyer requirements.

---

## 4. Conflict-Free Property Visit Scheduling
1. Navigate to **Visits** and click **Schedule Visit**.
2. Select the property, customer lead, and assigned consultant.
3. Select date, start time, and meeting duration.
4. **Conflict Validation:** If the agent already has a confirmed viewing during that timeframe, AqarFlow's backend conflict engine will warn you and reject the double booking.

---

## 5. Sales & Deal Execution
1. Navigate to **Deals**.
2. Create deal records linking buyer offer amounts and seller terms.
3. Once a deal reaches `Closed Won`, the system automatically marks the associated property as `Sold` or `Rented`.

---

## 6. Team & Roster Management
- Navigate to **Agents** to invite new property consultants, monitor their active lead workloads, or temporarily deactivate accounts.
